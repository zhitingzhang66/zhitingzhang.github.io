Meet Zhiting.

Professional artistry, advanced musical training, and thoughtful pedagogy.

Zhiting Zhang is a professional cellist and educator whose work brings together performance, advanced musical training, and thoughtful pedagogy.

Professional training

At the Cleveland Institute of Music, Zhang studied cello with Richard Weiss of The Cleveland Orchestra, with specialized training in orchestral excerpts and chamber music. Her musical development has also included studies and masterclasses with internationally distinguished artists including Alisa Weilerstein, Mike Block, David Ying, and Ludwig Quandt, 1st Principal Cello of the Berlin Philharmonic.

Selected honors

Performance experience

Her performance experience spans solo, chamber, and orchestral settings, with appearances at Benaroya Hall in Seattle, Severance Music Center in Cleveland, Meydenbauer Center in Bellevue, and Zhongshan Concert Hall in Beijing.

Teaching identity

Her teaching draws on this professional training and performance experience, emphasizing strong technical foundations, musical understanding, independent learning, and expressive artistry. Zhiting is a member of the Music Teachers National Association (MTNA).
