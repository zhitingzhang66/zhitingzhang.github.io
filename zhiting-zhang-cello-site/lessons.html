Lesson Formats
01

45-Minute Private Lesson

In Person · One-on-One

A focused format for younger and developing students, with time for technique, repertoire, musicianship, and effective practice guidance.

02

60-Minute Private Lesson

In Person · One-on-One

A comprehensive format for students ready for greater depth in technique, repertoire, interpretation, musicianship, and performance preparation.

03

2-Hour Cello Ensemble

In Person · Small Group

A collaborative experience that strengthens listening, rhythm, intonation, ensemble awareness, and musical communication.

What Students Study

Technique

Freedom & Control

Healthy movement, sound production, bow control, intonation, shifting, scales, and technical development.

Repertoire

Depth & Expression

Suzuki, ABRSM, and RCM pathways alongside carefully chosen repertoire for each student.

Musicianship

Listen & Understand

Ear training, sight-reading, rhythm, score awareness, and independent practice skills.

Performance Preparation

Prepare & Communicate

Chamber music, cello ensemble, orchestral excerpts, youth orchestra preparation, auditions, and performances as appropriate.
