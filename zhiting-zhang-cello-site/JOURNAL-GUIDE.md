# Publishing a new Journal post

1. Make a copy of `journal-template.html` and rename it, for example `practice-routine.html`.
2. Replace the article title, date, category, headings, and body text.
3. In `journal.html`, replace one of the “coming soon” cards with your title and a link to the new file, for example: `<a href="practice-routine.html">Read article →</a>`.
4. Commit and push the changed files in GitHub Desktop. The new article will be live within a few minutes.

You can also send the article text and photos to Codex for help with publishing and design.
