Meet Zhiting

Cellist · Educator

Thoughtful, confident,
independent musicians.

Zhiting Zhang is a cellist and educator dedicated to developing thoughtful, confident, and independent musicians.

She received her Master of Music degree from the Cleveland Institute of Music, where she studied with Richard Weiss of The Cleveland Orchestra. Her performance experience spans solo, chamber, and orchestral settings, with appearances at Benaroya Hall and Severance Music Center.

Her teaching brings together healthy technique, musical sensitivity, and expressive freedom. Students are guided to understand not only how to play, but how to listen, think, and make informed musical choices of their own.

Zhiting is a member of the Music Teachers National Association (MTNA).

Lesson Formats

Private learning,
shared discovery.

Thoughtful formats for students at different stages, with no pressure to fit a one-size-fits-all path.

01

45-Minute Private Lesson

In Person · One-on-One

A focused format for younger and developing students, with dedicated time for technique, repertoire, musicianship, and effective practice guidance.

02

60-Minute Private Lesson

In Person · One-on-One

A comprehensive format for students ready for greater depth in technique, repertoire, interpretation, and musicianship. Recommended for intermediate and advanced students.

03

2-Hour Cello Ensemble

In Person · Small Group

A collaborative experience that strengthens listening, rhythm, intonation, ensemble awareness, and musical communication alongside private study.
