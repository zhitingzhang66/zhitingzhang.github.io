01 — What Students Develop

More Than Learning
the Cello.

Cello study is not only about learning notes and technique. It is an opportunity to develop the way a young musician listens, thinks, expresses, and learns.

Strong Technique

Freedom & Control

A healthy, efficient technical foundation gives students the freedom to focus on music rather than struggle with the instrument.

Musical Awareness

Listen & Understand

Students learn to listen critically to intonation, sound, phrasing, rhythm, and musical character—and gradually recognize what to improve on their own.

Independent Learning

Think & Practice

Students develop effective practice strategies and learn how to solve musical and technical problems with increasing independence.

Expressive Confidence

Communicate & Perform

Students are encouraged to develop their own musical ideas and the confidence to communicate them through performance.

02 — How Students Learn

A Complete Approach
to Cello Learning.

Every student develops differently. Instruction is thoughtfully adapted to each student’s age, experience, learning style, and musical goals.

Technique

Freedom & Control

Healthy posture and movement, sound production, bow control, intonation, shifting, scales, and technical development become tools for greater musical freedom.

Repertoire

Depth & Expression

Carefully selected repertoire supports technical development while introducing composers, styles, musical languages, and expressive possibilities.

Ear Training

Listen & Respond

Students develop sensitivity to pitch, tone, phrasing, rhythm, and musical character through active listening and self-evaluation.

Sight-Reading & Musicianship

Read & Understand

Sight-reading, rhythm, score awareness, and musical understanding help students become fluent, adaptable, and increasingly independent musicians.

03 — Beyond the Lesson

Meaningful goals,
shared music.

Musical growth happens not only in the practice room, but through meaningful experiences that give students something to work toward.

Depending on each student’s interests, level, and goals, opportunities may include:

Studio Recitals

Develop confidence and experience sharing music with an audience.

Youth Orchestras

Preparation for programs such as BYSO and SYSO, supporting ensemble skills and increasingly challenging experiences.

All-State & Auditions

Individualized preparation for WMEA All-State and school or regional auditions.

Competitions & Performances

Guidance in repertoire selection, preparation, and performance for interested students.

ABRSM & RCM

Structured examination pathways for families who value measurable milestones and long-term progression.

The goal is never to pursue achievements for their own sake, but to use meaningful goals and performance experiences as part of each student’s musical development.

04 — Meet Zhiting

Cellist · Educator

Thoughtful, confident,
independent musicians.

Zhiting Zhang is a cellist and educator dedicated to developing thoughtful, confident, and independent musicians.

She received her Master of Music degree from the Cleveland Institute of Music, where she studied with Richard Weiss of The Cleveland Orchestra. Her performance experience spans solo, chamber, and orchestral settings, with appearances at Benaroya Hall and Severance Music Center.

Her teaching brings together healthy technique, musical sensitivity, and expressive freedom. Students are guided to understand not only how to play, but how to listen, think, and make informed musical choices of their own.

Her goal is to give each student the technical foundation, musical understanding, and confidence to develop an individual voice—both on the cello and as a musician.

Zhiting is a member of the Music Teachers National Association (MTNA).

05 — Teaching Philosophy

“My goal is for students to need fewer instructions, not more.”

To hear what they want, understand how to achieve it, and develop the confidence to make their own musical decisions.

06 — Lesson Formats

In Person · One-on-One

In Person · Small Group

A collaborative experience that strengthens listening, rhythm, intonation, ensemble awareness, and musical communication alongside private study.
