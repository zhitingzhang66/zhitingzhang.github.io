The Studio

Beyond the
Lesson.

As students grow, lessons can also support meaningful musical goals beyond the studio.

Depending on each student’s interests and goals, opportunities may include:

Studio Recitals

Performance experience and confidence on stage.

Cello Ensemble

Shared music-making that builds listening, rhythm, intonation, and ensemble awareness.

Youth Orchestras

Preparation for programs such as BYSO and SYSO.

All-State & Auditions

Preparation for WMEA All-State and school or regional auditions.

ABRSM & RCM

Structured pathways for measurable musical progress.

Achievements are not the destination—they are opportunities for students to grow.
